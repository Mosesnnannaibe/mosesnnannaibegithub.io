      <div class="pub-title">

        Seasonal and Temporal Patterns of Urban Air Pollutants and Their Differential Influence on the Air Quality Index: A Comparative Empirical Study

      </div>

      <div class="pub-authors">

        <strong>Moses Nnanna Ibe</strong>; Chinoso Job

      </div>

      <div class="pub-journal">International Journal of Engineering Research and Applications · 2026</div>

      <p class="pub-abstract">

        This study investigates how seasonal and temporal variations in urban air pollutants — including particulate matter,

        nitrogen dioxide, and ground-level ozone — differentially shape the composite Air Quality Index across multiple urban

        environments. Through rigorous comparative empirical analysis, the research identifies key temporal drivers of air quality

        degradation with implications for environmental policy and public health intervention.
